How Replay Works
Use replay when a downstream missed files
1. Upload the exact CDR files the downstream is missing (get them from your archive or re-download from the source).
2. Select the downstream portal that needs to receive the files.
3. Click Replay Files. The system re-decodes each file and delivers the output only to the selected portal.
✓ Other portals are untouched. Ongoing mediation is unaffected.
